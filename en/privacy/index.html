Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Preamble

PASOLA SAS is committed to protecting the privacy of its visitors and the security of their personal data, in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the French "Informatique et Libertés" law of January 6, 1978, as amended.

This policy describes the conditions under which personal data is collected and processed on the website pasola.fr.

Data Controller

  • PASOLA, simplified joint-stock company (SAS)
  • Registered office: 60 rue François Ier, 75008 Paris, France
  • SIREN: 995 071 636
  • Contact: contact@pasola.fr

Data collected

Contact form (Section "Begin the conversation")

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Profile of the inquirer (private buyer, family office, press)
  • Message (if provided)

Newsletter subscription form

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Explicit consent to receive communications

Language preference cookie

  • The "pasola-lang" cookie stores the user's manual language choice (French or English), retained for 1 year. See Cookie Policy.

Technical data

Cloudflare, as host, processes technical data (IP address, timestamp, HTTP headers, user agent) for the purposes of website delivery and protection against attacks. This data is retained for limited durations (typically from a few hours to 30 days depending on the type of log).

Your rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Right of access
  • Right of rectification
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object
  • Right to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
  • Right to define directives concerning the storage, deletion, and communication of your data after death

To exercise any of these rights, please write to: contact@pasola.fr. A response will be provided within one month of receipt of your request, this period being extendable by two months in the case of complex requests.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the French Data Protection Authority (CNIL), 3 place de Fontenoy, 75007 Paris, cnil.fr.

Minors

This website is not intended for minors. No data is knowingly collected from minors. If a parent or guardian discovers that data concerning a minor has been transmitted without their consent, they may request its deletion at contact@pasola.fr.

Security

PASOLA SAS implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to preserve the security, confidentiality, and integrity of personal data: encryption of communications (HTTPS / TLS), restricted access to data, careful selection of sub-processors.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. Any substantive change will be signaled by updating the date at the top of the document.
