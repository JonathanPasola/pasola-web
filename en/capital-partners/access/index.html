Key terms

Purpose
Mutual non-disclosure and non-circumvention agreement relating to the PASOLA Signature Estate project (Sumba, Indonesia).
Confidentiality
Undertaking not to disclose, copy, use for any other purpose, nor discuss with third parties the confidential information shared by the other Party.
Duration
Five (5) years from the date of signature. Obligations survive any termination of commercial discussions.
Non-circumvention
For the duration of the agreement and three (3) years following its expiration, undertaking not to circumvent the other Party to deal directly with its identified counterparties.
Non-solicitation
For the duration of the agreement and twelve (12) months following its expiration, undertaking not to solicit identified employees, partners, or suppliers.
Remedies
In the event of breach, liquidated damages and injunctive relief. The harm is acknowledged as irreparable.
Electronic signature
Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS), the electronic signature has the same legal effect as a handwritten signature.
Governing law
