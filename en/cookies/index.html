Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file placed by a website on the visitor's device (computer, tablet, phone) when consulted. Cookies serve to remember information between visits : language preferences, display settings, session tokens, or to measure website traffic.

This policy specifies the cookies used on the website pasola.fr and their purposes.

Cookies set by pasola.fr

NamePurposeDurationCategory
pasola-lang Stores the user's manual language choice (French or English) so that automatic IP-based redirection no longer applies once the user has expressed their preference. 1 year Strictly necessary

To date, pasola.fr does not set any audience-measurement cookie (Google Analytics, Plausible, Matomo, etc.) nor any advertising cookie.

Third-party cookies set by integrated services

Certain features of the website rely on third-party services that may set their own cookies as soon as the user interacts with them:

ServiceWhereCookies set (by the third party)

Spotify AB Embedded playlist in the footer, cookies set on player display Spotify cookies: session, listening preferences

PASOLA SAS does not control the cookies set by these third parties. To learn more, please consult the privacy policies of Google (YouTube), Spotify, and Cloudflare.

How to manage your cookies

You may at any time configure your browser to accept, refuse, or delete cookies. Each browser offers a different procedure:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy and security
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Refusing the "pasola-lang" cookie does not prevent navigation on the website. It will simply have the effect of applying automatic IP-based language redirection on each new visit.

Consent banner

The introduction of a granular consent banner, in compliance with CNIL recommendations, is in preparation. This banner will allow the user to accept or refuse, by category of purpose, cookies that are not strictly necessary.

Further information

The French Data Protection Authority (CNIL) publishes useful information on cookies and how they work: cnil.fr, Cookies and trackers.
